Not a Substitute for Emergency Care

Tailored by Innate provides elective IV hydration and wellness infusions. Our services are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and are not a substitute for emergency medical care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.

Physician-Directed Protocols

All infusion protocols are designed and overseen by our medical director. Every session is administered in person by a licensed registered nurse.

Individual Results Vary

Responses to IV therapy vary by individual. Statements about the benefits of specific infusions reflect general wellness goals and are not guarantees of any particular outcome.

Health History & Contraindications

Certain health conditions, medications, allergies, and pregnancy may make IV therapy inappropriate or require modification of a protocol. You must disclose your full and accurate medical history during intake so our clinical team can determine whether a service is appropriate for you.
